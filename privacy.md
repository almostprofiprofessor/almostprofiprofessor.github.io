Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 16, 2026
What we collect. When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account's display name and open ID) from TikTok. The App also reads basic statistics about videos it has published, such as views, to improve future videos.
How it is used. This information is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to view their performance. It is not used for advertising.
Sharing. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
Storage and security. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not uploaded anywhere else.
Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings, under Security and permissions, then Apps. Revoking access stops all data collection, and stored tokens are deleted.
Children. The App is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.
